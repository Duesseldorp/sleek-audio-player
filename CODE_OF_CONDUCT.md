# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Criticise code, not people.

## What that means here

This project is a small, friendly place. Everyone is welcome regardless of
experience level, background, nationality, gender, sexual orientation,
disability, appearance, age or religion.

**Expected:**

- Stay respectful and constructive, especially in disagreement
- Assume good intent — most misunderstandings are just that
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer; this plugin deliberately stays small
- Remember that everyone here is spending their free time

**Not acceptable:**

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment of any kind
- Discriminatory or sexualised language and imagery
- Publishing other people's private information
- Deliberately derailing discussions or demanding attention aggressively

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact me privately at
**kontakt@duesseldorp.de**. Reports are handled confidentially and taken
seriously.

As the maintainer I may edit, hide or delete comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that violate this code, and block people from the project when
necessary. I'll always explain the reason when I do.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this repository — issues, pull requests,
discussions and commit messages — and to public spaces where someone
represents this project.
